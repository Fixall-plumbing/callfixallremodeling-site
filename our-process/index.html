Your rights under NJ law

In New Jersey, home improvement contracts over $500 must be in writing, and you get 3 business days to cancel after you receive your copy. The notice is in your contract.

Permits

We make sure every required permit is issued before work starts. Plumbing and electrical permits are filed by the trade doing that work.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Don’t see yours? Ask at your free consult.

Book my free consult

Do you take a deposit?

Yes, when you sign the contract. The payment schedule is in your proposal before you sign anything. Nothing is charged before then.

What if I want to change something mid-job?

Changes get written up and priced before we do them. No surprise bills at the end.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on the scope and on cabinet and material lead times. Your proposal includes a realistic schedule before work starts. We don't promise dates on a website.

What's the difference between design-build and a general contractor?

With design-build, one company handles the design and the build, so there's one point of contact and one proposal. The other route is hiring a designer or architect first, then shopping the plans to contractors.

Do you clean up?

Every day. Floors and furniture protected, work area broom-clean.

Can I cancel after I sign?

Yes, within 3 business days of getting your copy, under New Jersey law. The notice is in the contract.
