Remodeling in East Brunswick

The Old Bridge Historic District

East Brunswick has its own Old Bridge Historic District, not to be confused with Old Bridge Township next door. It's bounded by the South River, Chestnut Hill Cemetery, Route 18 and Old Bridge Turnpike, and it went on the State Register in 1975 and the National Register in June 1977.

Subdivisions and townhomes

East Brunswick's building peaked in the 1980s. 4 out of 5 homes are owner-occupied and 64% are single-family, with townhome communities in the mix.

Remodels that fit

A house in one of the 1950s or 1960s subdivisions is a good candidate for an opened-up kitchen. A 1980s home is a good candidate for a new primary bath. 57% of the housing here went up before 1980.

Permits in East Brunswick

The East Brunswick Construction Inspection Division is at 4 Harts Lane, open 7:30 AM to 3:30 PM. Phone: 732-390-6875.
